ACME Financial Corp - Q2 2024 Earnings Call Transcript

CEO John Smith:
Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining our Q2 2024 earnings call.

We delivered solid results this quarter, though we are beginning to see 
some headwinds. Total revenue came in at $4.0 billion, in line with our 
guidance of $3.9 to $4.1 billion. Earnings per share was $1.98, meeting 
analyst estimates of $1.98.

Commercial lending remained strong, growing 15% year over year. However 
we are starting to see early signs of stress in our consumer portfolio. 
Credit losses increased to 0.8% of total loans, up from 0.4% last quarter.

We are closely monitoring our consumer book and have tightened underwriting 
standards. We remain cautious about the second half of the year given rising 
interest rates and inflation pressures on consumers.

Looking ahead to Q3, we expect revenue in the range of $4.0 to $4.2 billion.

CFO Jane Doe:
Net interest margin was flat at 3.6% this quarter. CET1 capital ratio 
decreased slightly to 12.8% due to loan growth. We have paused our share 
buyback program given the uncertain environment.
