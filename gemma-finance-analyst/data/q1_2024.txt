ACME Financial Corp - Q1 2024 Earnings Call Transcript

CEO John Smith:
Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining our Q1 2024 earnings call.

I am very pleased to report an exceptional quarter. Total revenue came in 
at $3.8 billion, above our guidance of $3.5 to $3.7 billion. Earnings per 
share was $1.92, beating analyst estimates of $1.85 by $0.07.

Our commercial lending segment was the star of the quarter, growing 22% 
year over year. Consumer banking also showed strength, up 8% year over year.
Credit losses remained well controlled at 0.4% of total loans.

Looking ahead to Q2, we expect revenue in the range of $3.9 to $4.1 billion.
We are optimistic about the macroeconomic environment and see strong loan 
demand across all segments.
