ACME Financial Corp - Q3 2024 Earnings Call Transcript

CEO John Smith:
Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining our Q3 2024 earnings call.

I am pleased to report that we delivered solid results despite a challenging 
environment. Total revenue came in at $4.3 billion, above our guidance range 
of $4.0 to $4.2 billion. Earnings per share was $2.14, beating analyst 
estimates of $2.06 by $0.08.

Our commercial lending segment was the primary growth driver, up 18% 
year over year. We are seeing strong demand from mid-market corporates.

However, we are closely monitoring rising credit losses in our consumer 
portfolio, which increased to 1.2% of total loans, up from 0.8% last quarter. 
Macro uncertainty and higher interest rates continue to pressure our retail 
customers.

Looking ahead to Q4, we expect revenue in the range of $4.2 to $4.5 
billion. We remain cautious given the uncertain macroeconomic environment 
but are confident in our commercial banking franchise.
